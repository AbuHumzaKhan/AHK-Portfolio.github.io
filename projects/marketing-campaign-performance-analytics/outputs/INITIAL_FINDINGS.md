## Findings to investigate

1. **Highest channel ROAS:** Email at 17.26x, with $13,574.74 modeled spend and $234,313.39 attributed value.
2. **Lowest CPA campaign:** Lifecycle Email at $4.00 across 3,394 simulated conversions.
3. **Largest spend allocation:** Generic Search at $90,434.04. Review marginal returns and budget pacing rather than judging on volume alone.
4. **Channel mix matters:** Paid Search accounts for the largest spend, but its modeled ROAS is below Email and Affiliate. A controlled test is more defensible than a broad reallocation.

## Recommended actions

- Test strong modeled-ROAS channels with a holdout where feasible.
- Investigate high-spend campaigns with below-portfolio ROAS.
- Compare CPA, conversion rate, conversion count, and order value together.
- Reconcile modeled spend and attributed value with actual billing and a consistent attribution window before operational use.

## Limitations

All values are synthetic and generated from campaign-specific assumptions. No actual ad platform, user-level identity, refunds, gross margin, multi-touch attribution, or incrementality experiment is represented. These descriptive scenario results do not establish causal campaign lift.
