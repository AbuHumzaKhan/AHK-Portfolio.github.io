# Assumptions and Limitations

1. Data is entirely synthetic and not an operational benchmark.
2. Unmet demand is treated as lost sales; backorders and substitutions are not modeled.
3. Replenishment is a teaching heuristic, not a cost-optimal policy.
4. The annual carrying-cost rate of 20% is an explicit illustrative assumption.
5. Potential lost sales value uses synthetic list price, not net sales, margin, tax, or recognized revenue.
6. Returns, transfers, shrinkage, promotions, cancellations, and financing are excluded.
7. The period boundary leaves some POs open; on-time rate is calculated only for received POs.
8. Differences across categories or warehouses are not causal evidence about real operations.
9. Validate demand and lead-time distributions against real history before changing reorder policies.
